Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who uses this app

Gmail auto-reply is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account. It is not available to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads the sender, subject and content of incoming emails to check them against the owner's rules. When a message matches, it sends a predefined reply and adds a label to that message.

How the data is handled

Emails are processed on the owner's own computer and are not stored, sold or shared with anyone. The access token that lets the app connect to Gmail is stored only on that computer. No data is used for advertising or analytics.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
